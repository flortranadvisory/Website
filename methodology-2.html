Our Approach

We built a full 3-statement financial model and ran scenario, pricing, and cost structure analysis to assess feasibility and maximize investor confidence.

01 Market & Concept Assessment

Assessed market size, demand drivers, competitive landscape, and concept fit for the island market.

02 Model Build (3-Statement)

Developed integrated income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement with key operating assumptions.

03 Scenario & Sensitivity Analysis

Tested multiple scenarios and key sensitivities to understand risk, upside potential, and break-even drivers.

04 Funding & ROI Assessment

Evaluated funding requirements, return on investment, and payback periods across different scenarios.

05 Deliver Insights & Recommendations

Delivered a clear feasibility outlook with actionable recommendations for stakeholders.

Key Methodological Frameworks

We used proven financial modeling and analysis techniques to ensure accuracy, transparency, and decision readiness.

3-Statement Financial Model

Integrated model linking income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow to capture the full financial picture.

Scenario Analysis

Constructed best case, base case, and downside scenarios to evaluate performance across different market conditions.

Sensitivity Analysis

Tested key variables including pricing, COGS, labor, and volume to identify the biggest impact drivers.
